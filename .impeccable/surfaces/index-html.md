# Surface brief — dt.parsons.edu home

Mode: persuade. Audience: prospective BFA/MFA students plus faculty, industry, and alumni. One job in one visit: understand what Design & Technology is, see whose program it is, take one action (apply / curriculum / thesis archive).

## Direction contract

THESIS: the page is an executed chance score, not a document — what is drawn is decided by chance at load time, and the program's facts sit in it as printed instructions. It refuses the university home page (hero statement, three link columns, faculty photo grid).

OWN-WORLD: newsprint ground `#E4E2DB`, ink `#14171A`, red pencil `#C2255C` reserved for instructions and the action. Two type voices only: an expanded heavy poster grotesque (Archivo, wdth 125) for lettering, and a typewriter face (Courier Prime) for instructions and captions. Ruled measures, marginal numbering, a printed cast block. No cards, no photography, no gray mid-tones except printed ink.

STORY: the visitor understands the program is critical about technology *with* technology since 1999, believes it because the page itself performs computational media and because 16 thesis years are listed as a real record, and does one thing: apply, read the curriculum, or open an archive year.

FIRST VIEWPORT: full-bleed score sheet. Masthead across the top at 9–12vw in expanded lettering: PARSONS DESIGN & TECHNOLOGY. The About line set as the score's premise directly under it, 65–75ch. The primary action — Apply — is an instruction block in the red-pencil margin, same visual weight as the premise, plainly clickable, plus "come visit us on D12" beneath it. Behind and through it: the live canvas of chance marks, drawing itself. Bottom of the fold carries the printed cast line: score number, seed, and the six-line I Ching cast.

FORM: position 2 of the seven grounded directions (1: Algorithmic Art Catalog, 2: Chance Operations Score). Chosen from the pick card over the assigned roll.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
